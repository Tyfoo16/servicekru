Vision & Mission

A Tech-Enabled Managed Platform

Our Vision

Empowering skilled talent and building the digital infrastructure for next-generation service ecosystems across South-East Asia.

Our Mission

Bridging Education, Digital Certification, and Workplace Placement to provide businesses and homes with standardised, reliable workforce solutions.

Industry Challenges

Built to Solve Real Industry Pain Points

F&B / Commercial

High turnover rates, shortage of job-ready talent, and excessive recruitment & training cost.

M&E / Technical Repair

Lack of standardisation, unverified credentials, and absence of liability insurance.

Workforce & Social Impact

Limited upskilling channels, lack of stable income, and low social recognition.

ESG & Social Impact

Empowering lives through meaningful work.

Women Career Elevation

Enabling women across Malaysia to gain sustainable income and build long-term, dignified careers.

Modernising the Gig Economy

Elevating informal labour into insured, digitally certified, and standardised service professionals.

ServiceKru bridges the gap from raw labour to certified professionals — building dignified careers for workers.

The E2W Engine

From Education to Work

E2W (Education-to-Work) is our three-step engine that turns raw talent into placed, certified professionals.

1

Vocational Education

Standardised training across F&B, M&E Engineering, Professional Care, and Home Care services.

2

Digital Certification

Tamper-proof Digital Career Passports issued through Accredible and anchored to the blockchain, paired with occupational insurance coverage.

Powered by Accredible
3

Smart OS Dispatch

Smart dispatch matching trained workforce with commercial (B2B) and residential (B2C) needs.
