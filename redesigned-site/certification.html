Digital Certification

The ServiceKru Digital Certification

Every ServiceKru professional is trained and certified before their first placement — issued through Accredible and backed by accredited training partners and our own internal assessment programme.

How It Works

Two Training Pathways

Every ServiceKru Digital Certification is backed by real training — delivered one of two ways, depending on the service line.

Accredited Training Partners

For cleaning professionals, training is delivered by accredited external partners such as MECSc under MCPC accreditation — structured courses, HRD Corp claimable.

Internal Training & Assessment

For other service lines, professionals are trained and assessed through ServiceKru's own internal programme.

Cleaning Certification

Cleaning Certification Pathway — via MECSc

Four structured levels of MCPC, Malaysia's nationally recognised cleaning certification — delivered through our accredited training partner, MECSc.

MCPC 1

Cleaning Fundamentals

  • Proper mopping, vacuuming & buffing
  • Chemical safety & equipment care
  • Washroom, window & wall cleaning standards
3 Days · RM1,900 ✓ HRD Corp Claimable
MCPC 2

Floor & Interior Maintenance

  • Machine scrubbing & floor polishing
  • Strip, reapply & maintain floor emulsion
  • High-level & exterior surface cleaning
2 Days · RM1,900 ✓ HRD Corp Claimable
MCPC 3

Carpet & Upholstery Care

  • Spray extraction & dry foam shampooing
  • Upholstery & carpet deep cleaning
  • Professional stain removal techniques
2 Days · RM1,900 ✓ HRD Corp Claimable
MCPC 4

Floor Restoration

  • Vitrification (crystallization) of hard floors
  • Semi-permanent seal application & stripping
  • Polish application & long-term maintenance
2 Days · RM2,600 ✓ HRD Corp Claimable
